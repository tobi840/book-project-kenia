Subject: Crab-plover (Reiherläufer), Dromas ardeola. The Crab-plover is a medium-sized, strikingly pied wading bird characterized by a very upright posture, a long, extended neck, and exceptionally long, bluish-grey legs ending in partially webbed toes. The body plumage is predominantly pure white, contrasting sharply with a solid black mantle (back) and black primary flight feathers. Its most unmistakable feature is a massive, laterally compressed, heavy black bill that is exceptionally stout for a wader, explicitly evolved for crushing crab shells. In non-breeding plumage (as typically seen in Kenya during October), the otherwise white head and nape are heavily streaked with pale grey.
