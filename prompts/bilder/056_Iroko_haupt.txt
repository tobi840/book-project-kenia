Subject: Iroko (Milicia excelsa). The Milicia excelsa is a towering, deciduous emergent tree reaching up to 50 meters, distinguished by a massive, perfectly straight, cylindrical bole that remains devoid of branches for the first 20 to 25 meters. Its crown is broad and distinctly umbrella-shaped, composed of thick, horizontal primary limbs that support dark green, broadly elliptical leaves with a slightly downy texture on the underside. The bark exhibits an ashen-gray to dark brown hue, covered in a highly textured pattern of thick scales that flake off in distinct squarish sections.
