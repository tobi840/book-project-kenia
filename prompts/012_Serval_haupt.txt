Subject: Serval, Leptailurus serval. The serval is a slender, lightly built feline defined by its extreme proportions, featuring the longest legs relative to body size of any wild cat and an elongated neck that gracefully elevates its small, delicate head. Its coat is a pale tawny-yellow, heavily patterned with solid black spots that coalesce into striking longitudinal stripes along the neck and shoulders. The uniquely large, rounded ears are set high and close together, standing completely erect. The hind limbs are noticeably longer than the forelimbs, giving the standing cat a coiled, forward-leaning posture that terminates in a relatively short, ringed tail with a solid black tip.
