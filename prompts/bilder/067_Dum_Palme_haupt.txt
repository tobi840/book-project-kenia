Subject: Dum-Palme, Hyphaene compressa. The Hyphaene compressa rises as a solitary, robust pillar from the arid ground before bifurcating into a highly geometric, strictly dichotomous branching pattern. At approximately four to five meters high, the grey, deeply vertically fissured trunk splits symmetrically in a Y-shape, a process that repeats up to five or six times, creating a sprawling canopy of up to 64 distinct spherical crowns. Each terminal head is an explosive rosette of costapalmate leaves, presenting a stark olive-to-bluish-green contrast, while old, withered leaf sheaths stubbornly cling to the upper sections of the branches, giving the lower canopy an untidy, thatched appearance.
